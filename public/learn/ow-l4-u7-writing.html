📖 Fact & Opinion Writing
3 / 13
💬
In fact and opinion writing, you state your opinion and use facts to support it. An opinion is something you believe to be true. A fact is a piece of true information — a date, an event, or a name.
🔥 Warm Up — fact or opinion?
Q1. "An airplane flies through the air."
Q2. "An airplane is the most useful invention."
📐 A fact can be checked. An opinion is what a person thinks or believes — and not everyone has to agree.
📖 Read the Model — "A Good Idea"
💬
Read it twice. The first time, just understand it. The second time, look for the facts that support each opinion.
¶1 — the opinion, then the facts
A Good Idea
In my opinion, sticky notes are a great invention. They’re easy to use. You write a note and stick it on your notebook or on your computer. And they come off easily. I think most people like them because they help you to remember things.
Q3. Which sentence is the writer’s OPINION?
Q4. Which FACT supports "they’re easy to use"?
¶2 — the history, all facts 📜
In my opinion, the story of sticky notes is interesting, too. Sticky notes have two inventors. Spencer Silver invented the glue in 1970. It wasn’t strong, so he didn’t know how to use it. But four years later, Arthur Fry found a use for it. One day, all his notes fell on the floor. He wasn’t happy! Then he remembered Silver’s glue! He used the glue on small pieces of paper. It worked! You could stick the notes to almost any surface, and it was easy to remove them. Now we have a cool—and useful—invention!
Q5. Who invented the glue, and when?
Q6. Why are dates and names so useful in this kind of writing?
📐 The shape to copy: OPINION first → then the FACTS that support it.
💬 Opinion Phrases
4 / 13
💬
Every opinion paragraph should open with one of these. They tell the reader: this is what I think.
📋 And for facts: It’s true that… · Many people use… · ___ invented the ___ in ___.

"___ Spencer Silver invented the glue in 1970." Which fits?

Before you call it finished — check the pronouns, then run the planner’s four-point check.
🔁 Pronoun & antecedent
A pronoun takes the place of a noun. The noun it replaces is the antecedent. They must agree in number and gender.
"Jared found ___ book."
"Luisa opened ___ backpack."
"Jared and Luisa rode ___ bikes."
