Dad: A cause is WHY something happens. An effect is WHAT happens. Read each part and tap the right button!
EXAMPLE
"I practise shooting every day" → 🔵 CAUSE (this is what I DO)
"I score more goals in matches" → 🟢 EFFECT (this is what HAPPENS)
