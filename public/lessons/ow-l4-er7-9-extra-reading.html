✏️ Design — noun or verb?

Design works two ways. Which sentence uses it as a verb (an action)?

design

“He made a design for a flying machine.”

🏷️ Label It

Leonardo wrote small notes next to each part of his drawings. Those notes are labels. Why label a drawing?

🏷️
label
noun / verb
a small piece of writing that names a part — or to add one
“Label the parts of your invention.”

🔎 Be the Expert — Text Features

Text features are the parts of a page that help you understand it.

🅱️ PRINT

bold, italics, underlining — they make you notice important words.

🖼️ GRAPHICS

photos, drawings, maps — they help you picture the information.

📊 INFORMATIONAL

timelines, captions, bullet points — they help you understand new information.

🗂️ ORGANIZATION

title, subtitle, headings, glossaries — they help you find things and connect ideas.

👤 Be the Expert — Elements of a Biography

A biography tells the life of a real person.

1
Factual information — place and date of birth. 📍
2
Places and events that changed the person’s life. 🗺️
3
Achievements — what they did that mattered. 🏆
🎯 Formative check: can Leo give five facts about Leonardo’s life? Use the text and the timeline.

📅 The timeline — part 1 (chronological = by date)

1452
Leonardo da Vinci is born in Anchiano, Italy. 🇮🇹
1467
Young Leonardo goes to Florence to study with the artist Andrea del Verrocchio. 🎨
1482
He goes to Milan to work for the city’s duke as a painter and engineer. 🏛️
1500
He returns to Florence and studies mathematics. ➗
💬 LP: The information in a timeline is organized chronologically, that is, by date.

📅 The timeline — part 2

1502
He works for Cesare Borgia as an architect, map-maker and engineer. 🗺️
1503
He begins his most famous painting, Mona Lisa. 🖼️
1503
–1513
He studies flight, the human body, and rocks and plants. 🕊️
1516
–1519
He moves to France. He dies there. 🇫🇷

🧮 So Leonardo lived from 1452 to 1519. How old was he? Work it out! (67)
