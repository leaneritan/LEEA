Units 7–9

Showing interest  ·  Asking about words  ·  TR 9.12–9.14

🤔 You don’t know a word. What do you do?

This happens in every single lesson. There is a right answer — and this page gives you the words for it.

In this lesson, I will…

1
show I am interested with a short question — Do you?
2
react to good news — Wow. That’s so cool!
3
ask about a word three different ways.
4
say I am not sure — politely — and suggest what to do.
🎯 Nothing on this page is for the test. Every phrase is for real use, starting in the next lesson.

Two jobs, one page

Job 1 — keep it going

🙌 Show interest

The other person says something. You answer in a way that makes them want to say more.

Do you?What about you?Wow. That’s so cool!
Job 2 — get unstuck

🙋 Ask for help

You are missing a word. You get it without leaving English.

What does this word mean?How do you spell…?How do you say… in English?

Three questions about words

You see it, you don’t know it

🔤 What does ___ mean?

「どういう意味？」

You know it, you need the letters

✏️ How do you spell ___?

「どう書くの？」

You know it in Japanese

🗣️ How do you say ___ in English?

「英語で何て言うの？」

You want to write creativity, but you don’t know the letters. Which one?

🤷 “I don’t know” — and one that disagrees

When you don’t know
I don’t know.I’m not sure.No idea.

No idea is the casual one — fine with a friend, not with a teacher.

When you disagree
Um, I don’t think so.

That little Um is not an accident. It softens the whole sentence.

Martina says “I think it’s a kind of invention.” Antoni does not agree. What does he say?

💡 “Why don’t you…?” is not a question

It looks like a question. It means “here is an idea.” Tap each situation.

🙋
Leo doesn’t know. Dad might.
🤷🤷
Neither of them knows.
👍
Someone suggests it. You agree.
🔑 you = you do it  ·  we = we do it together. One word, completely different plan.
