📐 Grammar 1 of 4 — used to (Unit 7)

“How did people use to tell the time before they had clocks?”
“They used to look at the sun.” ☀️

⚠️ In the question

use to — no d! The did already made it past.

✔️ In the answer

used to — with d.

💬 This is the grammar Activity 3 needs. Get it solid here and Activity 3 runs itself.

✏️ Three ways to write a definition

1 · Say what it means

“It’s a force which pulls you toward the earth.” 🌍

2 · Give an example

“Collecting fossils is an example of this.” 🦴

3 · Give the opposite

“It’s the opposite of push.” 🫴

Activity 6 · the game

🎲 Read and guess

Read your definition out loud.
Do not say the word.
Can the other person guess it? 🤔

Dad’s model

“This is the opposite of forward.”

→ backward ⬅️

Scoring

1 point for a correct guess.
1 point to the writer if the definition was clear. ⭐

📖 Be the Expert — Teaching Tip

Students often have to read a text more than once to really understand it.

1
Give time to read silently first.
2
Let him underline words he does not know.
3
Review those words before he answers anything.
4
Answer his language questions before starting.
💬 This is why Activity 4 says “scan and underline” before answering. Do not skip that step.
