Q36–37 · Full sentences — 2 points each

QUESTION 36 · 2 POINTS

What is one thing old-fashioned skateboards used to have? What thing didn’t they use to have?

✔ Old-fashioned skateboards used to have four wheels. They didn’t use to have batteries / a motor / controllers.

QUESTION 37 · 2 POINTS

What do you use the controller on an electric skateboard for?

✔ You use it to go fast or slow / to brake.

🎯 Both ask for a complete sentence. Give 2 for a correct full sentence, 1 when the content is right but it is a fragment, 0 when the content is wrong.

Q41 · Writing — 10 points

PROMPT A

A hobby he likes — 3 to 4 sentences on why it is his favourite and why it is interesting, using for example and such as.

PROMPT B

His favourite invention, including his opinion of it, using in my opinion, I think, I believe.

FOUR CRITERIA · 2.5 / 2 / 1.5 / 1 EACH
The task. He writes about the hobby (a) or the invention (b) and uses the words the prompt asks for.
Theme. The topic is clear and he stays on it.
Vocabulary. Words are used correctly.
Grammar. Grammar is correct.
🔢 Add the four up and round to a whole number on the score screen’s 0–10 row.
