# Design notes — Unit 4 Grammar 1 — Advice with should (`ow-l4-u4-grammar-1.html`)

## The lesson

- Our World Level 4, Unit 4 *Get Well Soon!* · SB pp. 66–67 · TR 4.4 · LP pp. 142–145 (PDF 142–145) · WB p. 45 · GWB pp. 17–18
- Objectives (LP p. 142, verbatim): give advice with should · talk and write about treating health problems
- Target pattern: **subject + should / shouldn’t + base verb** (the same for every person), **Should + subject + base verb?** and **What should + subject + do?** with short answers
- Academic Language: contraction · recycled: Vocabulary 1 (fever, toothache, cough, feel dizzy, tissues…)
- **Hard scope line:** should / shouldn’t only. No must, have to or could; reflexive pronouns wait for Grammar 2.

## The spine — Dr. Leo’s Clinic

Leo runs a clinic. Patients arrive with aches, colds and casts, and the only way to help one is to give the right advice with *should* or *shouldn’t* — a wrong form helps nobody. Each patient helped goes up on the clinic wall that every spine slide shows and that keeps growing (saved in the deck’s own storage). The payoff slide is the full wall, with Leo writing three prescriptions in the SB Activity 3 pattern: what is wrong + should + shouldn’t.

## The two bars

*Detailed*: the subject is swapped one press at a time — I → you → he → they — and *should use* never moves; “he shoulds uses” is shown wrong first before the fix. *should not* is squeezed into *shouldn’t* in steps, then given its good-advice partner. *Creative*: every right piece of advice is a patient helped, so the wall is the visible accumulation of correct sentences.

## JS safety

One script block; data passed as JSON (no apostrophes inside onclick); no confirm(); two-tap clear; widgets guard every DOM read.

## Soccer freshness

Players by position only (the striker with a cast, the dizzy keeper) — no player, club or statistic is asserted, so nothing dates.
