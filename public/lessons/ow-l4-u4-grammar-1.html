contraction
/kənˈtrækʃən/ con-trac-tion
noun
a short form made by putting two words together and replacing letters with an apostrophe
🇯🇵 短縮形（たんしゅくけい）
💬 Example: Shouldn't is a contraction of should not.

Unit 4·The Grammar BoxTR 4.4
🎧
Advice with should. “When we tell others what they should do, we’re giving advice.”
TR 4.4
I have a bad toothache.
What should I do?
You shouldn’t wait any longer.
Your mom should take you to the dentist.
📐 Use should to give advice
FormPatternExample
Positive advicesubject + should + base verbYou should take your medicine.
Negative advicesubject + shouldn’t + base verbHe shouldn’t go to school.
Yes/No questionShould + subject + base verb?Should I stay home?
WH questionWhat should + subject + do?What should I do?
Rule: Should stays the same for I, you, he, she, we, and they. Use the base form after should.

Unit 4·Advice Detective 🕵️
🕵️ Every sentence has ONE mistake. Pick the fixed version.
🇯🇵 Japanese bridge: 〜したほうがいい = should + 動詞の原形. 〜しないほうがいい = shouldn’t + 動詞の原形. No to, no -s, no -ing after should.
