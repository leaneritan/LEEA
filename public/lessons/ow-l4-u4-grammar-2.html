📐 Reflexive pronouns
FormPatternExample
II + verb + myselfI cut myself.
youyou + verb + yourselfDon’t hurt yourself.
hehe + verb + himselfHe burned himself.
sheshe + verb + herselfShe hurt herself.
we / theywe/they + verb + ourselves/themselvesThey hurt themselves.
Rule: Use a reflexive pronoun when the subject and object refer to the same person or people. By + reflexive pronoun means alone or without help.

Unit 4·Grammar in Depth — Same Person or Different?Lesson Planner pp. 148–149
🔍 We use a reflexive pronoun when the subject and the object are the same person.
Eva is teaching John to speak Spanish.
She is teaching him to speak Spanish.

Eva is teaching herself to speak Spanish. 🪞
Lesson Planner p. 149 · Grammar in Depth (examples verbatim)
Unit 4·Grammar in Depth — by + myself = aloneLesson Planner pp. 148–149
🙋 When by comes before a reflexive pronoun, it means alone, without help.
My brother shouldn’t cook by himself. (= alone, without help.) Is it “my own body” or “alone”?
🙋 I cut myself = I cut my own body. I made the first-aid kit by myself = I made it alone. No one helped me.
Lesson Planner p. 149 · Grammar in Depth · Workbook p. 47 “Look at the difference”
Unit 4·Mirror Detective 🕵️
🕵️ Every sentence has ONE mistake. Pick the fixed version.
🇯🇵 Japanese bridge: 自分を／自分で = -self. 「自分で切ってしまった」 = I cut myself. 「一人で」 = by myself. The -self word must match the person: he → himself (never hisself).
