Unit 4·Vocabulary Strategy — SuffixesBe the Expert
🧩 A suffix is a word part added to the end of a base word. Watch the word change.
-ness = a thing · -ish = a little like · -able = can be · -en = to become · -ful = full of · -less = without
🧩 Our bones are breakable = Our bones can break.
