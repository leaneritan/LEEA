# Design notes — Unit 5 Grammar 1 — Superlatives with -est and most (`ow-l4-u5-grammar-1.html`)

## The lesson

- Our World Level 4, Unit 5 *My Favorites* · SB pp. 82–83 · TR 5.4 · LP pp. 172–175 (PDF 172–175) · WB p. 57 · GWB pp. 20–21
- Objectives (LP p. 172, verbatim): use superlatives with -est and most · use comparatives and superlatives to compare photos of people and things · compare opinions
- Target pattern: **the + adjective-est** (one syllable; two syllables ending in y → -iest) / **the most + adjective** (-ous, -ful and three-or-more syllables), plus the group phrase (in the world · ever · of all)
- Academic Language: compare · Content Vocabulary: opinion, world · recycled: all 15 Vocabulary 1 words
- **Hard scope line:** superlatives only. Comparatives (-er / more) appear only where the Student Book itself uses them (Activity 2's three pictures). No irregular forms — good / bad are Grammar 2.

## The spine — LEEA Awards Night

Leo hosts an awards night. Every award has a superlative name (*The Most Amazing Singer*, *The Funniest Movie*), so the only way to create an award is to build the superlative correctly — a wrong form pins nothing. Each award goes on a trophy wall that every spine slide shows and that keeps growing (saved in the deck's own storage), and the payoff slide is the full wall with Leo's host speech: "And the award for the funniest movie goes to…".

## The two bars

*Detailed*: every form is built in steps Leo presses one at a time — great → great**est** → **the** greatest → the greatest fairy tale **ever**; funny → funn~~y~~ → funn**i** → funni**est** → **the** funniest. *Talented* is shown wrong first (talented~~est~~ ❌) before **most** fixes it. *Creative*: the award names are the grammar, so the structure is load-bearing; the wall is the visible accumulation.

## Emoji map

⚖️ compare (compare three animals, pick the top one) · 💭 opinion (fact or opinion sort) · 🌍 world (find the group phrases) · 🏆 awards

## JS safety

One script block; data passed as JSON (no apostrophes inside onclick); no confirm(); two-tap clear; widgets guard every DOM read.

## Soccer freshness

Award names only (the greatest striker, the bravest keeper…) — no player, club or statistic is asserted, so nothing dates.
