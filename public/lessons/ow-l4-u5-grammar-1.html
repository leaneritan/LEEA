📐 Superlatives with -est and most
FormPatternExample
One syllablethe + adjective-estHe's the greatest basketball player in the world.
Two syllables ending in ythe + adjective-ies + tShe's the funniest person in our class.
Two or more syllablesthe most + adjectiveIt's the most popular show on TV.
Rule: Use superlatives to compare one person or thing with all the others. Use phrases such as in the world, ever, and of all to show the comparison group.

Unit 5·Superlative Detective 🕵️
🕵️ Every sentence has ONE mistake. Pick the fixed version.
🇯🇵 Japanese bridge: 一番〜 = the + -est / the most + word. Never both: the most funniest. Don’t forget the: He is tallest → He is the tallest.
grammar.json note: do not use both most and -est together
