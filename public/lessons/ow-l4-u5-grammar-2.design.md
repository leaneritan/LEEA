# Design notes — Unit 5 Grammar 2 — Irregular comparatives and superlatives (`ow-l4-u5-grammar-2.html`)

## The lesson

- Our World Level 4, Unit 5 *My Favorites* · SB p. 85 · TR 5.6 · LP pp. 178–179 · WB p. 59 · GWB pp. 22–23 · cutouts SB p. 175
- Objective (LP p. 178, verbatim): use irregular comparatives and superlatives: better, the best, worse, the worst
- Academic Language: comparison · Content Vocabulary: health, music
- **Hard scope line:** good → better → the best and bad → worse → the worst only. The Activity 2 cutouts mix in six regular adjectives (amazing, popular…), which the book itself pairs with the nouns — those questions use Grammar 1 forms and are labelled as such.

## The spine — Leo's Top & Flop Board

Every thing Leo rates with *better / the best* is pinned to the TOP; every thing rated with *worse / the worst* goes to the FLOP side. A wrong form ("more better", "gooder") pins nothing. The board is visible on each spine slide and keeps filling; the payoff is Leo's own best-and-worst list (GWB p. 23).

## The two bars

*Detailed*: "We don't add -er to good" is shown, not told — good~~er~~ ❌ → better → better **than** my friend → **the best** in our class. *Creative*: only the irregular forms can pin a card, and the board is the accumulation.
