We have to keep the oceans clean.
Use have to and must to talk about obligation. Use can't and don't to talk about prohibition.
subject + have to / must + base verb | can't / Don't + base verb
🇯🇵 have toとmustは「〜しなければならない」という義務を表します。can'tとdon'tは「〜してはいけない」という禁止を表します。
Watch out: We have to keep the oceans clean. = We must keep the oceans clean. (same meaning; must is stronger/more formal)

Unit 6·Be the Expert
must vs. have to
They mean the same thing here, but must is stronger and more formal, and have to is far more common in speaking. Leo should be able to produce both — and should default to have to when he is talking.
🇯🇵 主語 + have to / must + 動詞の原形 ／ can't / Don't + 動詞の原形
