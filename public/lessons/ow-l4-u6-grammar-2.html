Unit 6·Be the Expert
won't = will not
won't is the contraction of will not — the spelling is irregular, so Leo has to learn it as a word, not build it. In short answers we never contract the positive: Yes, they will (never “Yes, they’ll”).
🇯🇵 主語 + will / won't + 動詞の原形
