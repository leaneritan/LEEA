Unit 6·Think. Pair. Share.How it works
🧠 → 👥 → 📣 Three steps, every question.
🧠
Think
Quiet. Thirty seconds. No talking — Leo needs the thinking time.
👥
Pair
Say it to Dad. Half-finished sentences are fine here.
📣
Share
Say the finished answer out loud, in one clear sentence.
Do not skip Pair. It is where the sentence gets fixed, and it is the only step where a mistake costs nothing.
Student Book p. 104 · Think. Pair. Share.
