Unit 6·Be the Expert — Our World in Context
How deep is the ocean?
The sunlit zone reaches about 200 m. The twilight zone reaches about 1,000 m. Below that is the midnight zone — and the deepest place in the ocean, the Mariana Trench, is about 11,000 m deep. Mount Fuji is only 3,776 m tall. It would disappear completely.
