an oil spill
/ɔɪl spɪl/
oil spill
Noun
when oil escapes from a ship or pipe into the ocean
🇯🇵 石油流出（せきゆりゅうしゅつ）— 船やパイプから石油が海にもれてしまうこと。
💬 Dad: "Big ships called tankers carry oil. When one has an accident, the oil goes straight into the sea."
🔊 TR 6.5 says: "There was a bad oil spill near the beach, so we can't swim there."
0 / 3
🎉 3 / 3!
How do we use it?
  • "There was a bad oil spill near the beach."
  • Two words: oil + spill. To spill is to let something fall out by accident.

/ˈoʊvərˌfɪʃɪŋ/

Noun
catching too many fish so there aren't enough left to reproduce
🇯🇵 乱獲（らんかく）— 魚をとりすぎて、海に残らなくなること。

How do we use it?
  • "We need to stop overfishing."
  • over- = too much: overeating, oversleeping, overfishing.
