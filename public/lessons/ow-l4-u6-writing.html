🐢 Land and Sea Turtles
All turtles begin their lives on land. Mother turtles lay their eggs in holes and then cover them up to protect them. after that, the baby turtles’ lives are very different. The baby land turtles crawl away to live in woods, swamps, grasslands, or deserts. , the baby sea turtles crawl to the ocean to live their lives in the water.
Land turtles and sea turtles look different, too. Land turtles have hard, high, round shells. When they are afraid, they hide in their shells. Sea turtles, , have soft, flatter shells. They can’t hide inside, they can swim away really fast. When cold weather comes, land turtles dig holes in the ground and sleep. They are too slow to move to warmer places. , sea turtles simply swim away to find warmer waters.

Unit 6·Contrast WritingSB p. 104
The book's own definition — read it out loud
In contrast writing, you write about the differences between two things. You can use facts and descriptive words to show differences. You can also use words that show contrast, such as but and however, and expressions such as in contrast.
Two halves. First: facts and descriptive words — that is the content. Second: but, however, in contrast — that is the joinery. A paragraph needs both, and Leo will usually bring only the first.

Unit 6·Be the Expert — Where the Comma Goes
The commas are the hard part
but takes a comma before it, and nothing after: They can't hide inside, but they can swim away really fast.

however takes a comma on each side: Sea turtles, however, have soft, flatter shells.

In contrast starts a new sentence and takes a comma after it: In contrast, sea turtles simply swim away.
Quick check for Leo: can he say which one needs two commas? That is the one he will forget.
