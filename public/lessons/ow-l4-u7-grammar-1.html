Unit 7·Grammar in Depth — Two Flavours📖 LP p.239 · Be the Expert
① A past state
Something that was true for a period of time, but isn't now.
I used to have long hair.
Now I have short hair.
② A past action
Something that happened regularly, but doesn't now.
I used to walk to school.
Now I ride my bike.
🔗 It is common to join the two halves with but: I used to walk to school, but now I ride my bike.

Unit 7·Time Machine Rules Board📋 summary
1used to + plain verb — a past habit that stopped. People used to travel by boat.
2didn't use to + plain verb — the negative. We didn't use to have airplanes.
3Did … use to + plain verb? — the question. Why did people use to read by candlelight?
4After didn't and did, used loses its -d. They already show the past.
5used to is the same for I / you / he / she / we / they. It never changes.
6Join past and present with but now: I used to walk, but now I ride.

Unit 7·Be the Expert — Glides 🌬️📖 LP p.241 · Sounds of English
/w/  as in wash
The tongue starts high at the back of the mouth (like /uː/) and glides to an open position.
wash · walk · we · why · world
/j/  as in young
The tongue starts high at the front of the mouth (like /iː/) and glides to an open position.
young · you · year · yes · used to
🗣️ used starts with a /j/ glide — say "yoost", not "oozed". Practice: you · young · used to. Cards 23 & 24.
