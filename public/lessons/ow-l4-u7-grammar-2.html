🔑 The swap test: if you can put people in place of you and the sentence still makes sense, it is the general you.

Unit 7·Grammar in Depth — Three Jobs for "you"📖 LP p.245 · Be the Expert
🙋 Specific you
One person, or one group you are speaking to.
Marc, where are you from?
You are good students.
🌐 Impersonal you
People in general. Used to ask for information, to explain how to do something, and to make general statements.
How do you play this game?
First, you install it on your phone.
You should eat breakfast every morning.
🔄 "How do you play this game?" = "How do people play this game?" — same meaning.

Unit 7·Invention Guide — Rules Board📋 summary
1The general you = people in general, not one person.
2The swap test: if people fits in place of you, it is the general you.
3Questions keep the do: What do you do with it? How do you use it?
4After you, the verb has no -s: you need, not you needs.
5One thing → it. More than one thing → them. You color pictures with them.
6Start every clue with You.
