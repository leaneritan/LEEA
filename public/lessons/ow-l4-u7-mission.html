Unit 7·Be the Expert — Sheep View 360 🐑📷📖 LP p.253 · Book p.127
🐑📷
🇫🇴 The Faroe Islands · Northeast Atlantic Ocean
1The Faroe Islands are a group of 18 volcanic islands.
2The sheep carries a solar-powered camera, taking a photo every minute.
3The project is called Sheep View 360.
4Its purpose: map the islands so they appear on Google Street View.
5It worked — the islands are on street-view maps online today.
Unit 7·Problem → Solution 🔗the unit in one photo
❓ What was the PROBLEM?
Nobody had mapped the Faroe Islands. They were not on street-view maps.
✅ What was the SOLUTION?
Put solar-powered cameras on the sheep — they already walk everywhere!
💭 Where did it come from?
Imagination and creativity — exactly the Unit 7 mission.
🐑 Nobody gave them a mapping car. They used what they already had, in a new way.
Unit 7·Meet the Explorer — Aydogan Ozcan 🔬⚡ Power Station ②
"Science is entirely based on curiosity."
"In science, it's always a long train of ideas. Many succeed, but in between you often fail… science is entirely based on curiosity."
— Aydogan Ozcan, Electrical Engineer, National Geographic Explorer
📱→🔬 What he invented
His team at UCLA invented a way to turn cell phones into microscopes.
It can be carried to the most remote areas, so doctors can diagnose malaria and other deadly diseases there.
💡 What he believes
The future is not only new technology — it is using the technology we already have in new ways.
"That's what will transform global health care in powerful, practical ways we've never before imagined."
