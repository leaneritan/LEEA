Unit 7·Think Like a Superhero 🦸📗 Book p.128
Choose your own superpower — something that makes life better.
🎯 The app must be the solution to a problem. A pretty app that solves nothing is not finished.
🤔 How will it work?
Will it sound an alarm? ⏰
Will it send you a message? 💬
Will it show you a picture? 🖼️

Quick drawings — not perfect art. The idea matters more than the lines.

Unit 7·Give Feedback — Kindly 💬📖 LP pp.254-255
💬"Your app looks fun. However, I don't understand how it will…"
💬"I like your sketches, but I would maybe change the colors."
💬"The ideas are great, but I think you don't need that many."
🎁 Giving feedback
Do not change your partner's idea completely — make it work better. Be respectful.
🙏 Receiving feedback
Take it positively. Use the ideas to make the project better. Say thank you.
⚡ Feedback exchanged — tap to power Station ④.
