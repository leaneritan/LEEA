Unit 7·Be the Expert — Vocabulary Strategy
Using the suffix -ful
A letter or letters added to the end of a word is called a suffix. When a suffix is added to a word, it forms a new word with a different meaning. A suffix may also change a word's part of speech.

The suffix -ful means "full of", or "having the qualities of". Other vocabulary words that contain -ful include beautiful, colorful, and wonderful. Adding the suffix -ful to the words beauty, color, and wonder changes each word from a noun to an adjective.

Electricity around the world
Located on the eastern part of the continent of Asia, China has the largest population of any country on Earth. China also uses the greatest amount of electricity per year, as measured in kilowatt-hours. After China, the countries that use the most electricity per year are the United States, India, Japan, and Russia.
