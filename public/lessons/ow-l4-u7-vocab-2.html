Unit 7·Why "turns" and not "turn"?📖 LP p.243
You → turn
You move your waist.
You lift the hoop.
The subject is you — no -s.
The hoop → turns
The hoop turns around and around.
The wheels turn.
One hoop = it → add -s. But "the wheels" is plural → no -s on the verb!
🔑 Instructions to a person drop the subject: Put · Lift · Move. A sentence about a thing keeps it: The hoop turns.

Unit 7·Be the Expert — Instructions📖 LP p.243 · Vocabulary Strategy
✅ How to give an instruction
Start with the base form of the verb. Do not add the person (the subject). Keep the tone polite.
Write the answer in your notebook.
Raise your hand if you know the answer.
Don't use your phones in class.
🔢 Words that show sequence
Use these to put the steps in order.
First, crack three eggs in a bowl.
Then add one cup of sugar.
Next, mix for two minutes.
Finally, bake it.
🎯 Try it: give Dad a three-step instruction for something you know how to do. Use first, then, finally.

Unit 7·Our World in Context — The Hula Hoop📖 LP p.243
🪀🌺
Since ancient times, hoops have been used as toys.
The hula hoop is named after the Hawaiian dance hula 🌺, performed by moving the hips in a circular motion.
A similar motion keeps the hoop turning around the user's waist.
The hula hoop was popular around the world in the 1950s, but it is less popular today.
🔗 An old invention that people still use — exactly the Unit 7 big idea.
