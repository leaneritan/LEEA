Unit 7·Warm Up — Two Sentences📖 LP p.250
📋 FACT
"An airplane flies through the air."
Is this true? Yes — always. Something that is always true is a fact.
💬 OPINION
"An airplane is the most useful invention."
Is this true? Some people think so. An opinion is what a person thinks or believes.

Unit 7·Read the Model — "A Good Idea" ¶1📗 Book p.126 · Act 1
A Good Idea
In my opinion, sticky notes are a great invention. They're easy to use. You write a note and stick it on your notebook or on your computer. And they come off easily. I think most people like them because they help you to remember things.
🎮 Which FACT supports "they're easy to use"?
🟡 The highlighted parts are opinions. Everything between them is a fact that supports one.
Unit 7·Read the Model — ¶2, the history📗 Book p.126
In my opinion, the story of sticky notes is interesting, too. Sticky notes have two inventors. Spencer Silver invented the glue in 1970. It wasn't strong, so he didn't know how to use it. But four years later, Arthur Fry found a use for it. One day, all his notes fell on the floor. He wasn't happy! Then he remembered Silver's glue! He used the glue on small pieces of paper. It worked! You could stick the notes to almost any surface, and it was easy to remove them. Now we have a cool—and useful—invention!
🎮 Who invented the glue, and when?

Unit 7·What Counts as a Fact? 📋📖 LP p.250
📅A date — "Spencer Silver invented the glue in 1970."
🎬An event — "One day, all his notes fell on the floor."
🏷️A name — "Arthur Fry found a use for it."
🔢A number — "Sticky notes have two inventors."
🔑 An opinion on its own is weak. An opinion plus a fact is convincing.

Unit 7·Be the Expert — Pronouns 🔁📖 LP p.251 · Writing Support
🔁 A pronoun takes the place of a noun. The noun it replaces is the antecedent. They must agree in number and gender.
Jared found ___ book.
Luisa opened ___ backpack.
Jared and Luisa rode ___ bikes, and ___ had a lot of fun.
📝 In the model: "Sticky notes have two inventors. They…" — plural noun, plural pronoun. ✔
