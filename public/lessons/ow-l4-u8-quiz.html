Q8–12 · Join the two sentences with who

Example: I have a sister. She loves stuffed animals. → I have a sister who loves stuffed animals.

8️⃣ I have a grandmother. She loves to cook.
9️⃣ There is a girl in my class. She loves to dance.
1️⃣0️⃣ I have a cousin. He loves to study dinosaurs.
1️⃣1️⃣ I have an uncle. He likes computers.
1️⃣2️⃣ My best friend is a creative person. She makes videos.
🔧 who replaces the pronoun, so She and He disappear. If the pronoun survives — a grandmother who she loves to cook — the idea is right and the grammar is not.

Q13–17 · Rewrite with to or for

Example: I gave her a flower. → I gave a flower to her.

1️⃣3️⃣ My cousin sent me a letter.
1️⃣4️⃣ I made them a cake.
1️⃣5️⃣ My father bought him a sandwich.
1️⃣6️⃣ Don showed us the fossil.
1️⃣7️⃣ I gave you the notebook.
⚖️ The verb chooses the word: send, show, give → to  ·  make, buy → for. Three of these five take to, two take for, so a run of all-to answers is the thing to spot. The object moves too: sent me a letter → sent a letter to me.
