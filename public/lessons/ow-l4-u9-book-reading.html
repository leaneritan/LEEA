📜 What kind of story is a folktale?

A folktale is a very old story. Nobody knows who first told it — it was passed from person to person for hundreds of years before anyone wrote it down.

🦊 Be the Expert — a trickster tale

This folktale is a special kind called a trickster tale. In a trickster tale, a big person or animal is tricked by a smaller, smarter one.

🧠 The trickster

Small. Clever. Usually wins.

💪 The tricked

Big. Strong. Often does not even realise what happened!

💬 Keep this in your head while you read. Who do you think the trickster is going to be? 🐢

🪢 Pull, Pulled — Tug, ______?

Tugged simply means pulled. Which sentence uses it correctly?

🔁 Unit 9 words you already know: push ↔ pull.  Tug is a strong, sudden pull.  Tugged is the past.

🪢
tugged
verb (past)
pulled
“Elephant tugged, and Hippo tugged back.”
引(ひ)っぱった

🪢 Rope Rules

In a game of tug-of-war, how do the two teams stand?

🪢
tug-of-war
noun
a game in which two teams pull on opposite ends of a rope to try to move each other
“Turtle wanted a tug-of-war.”
綱引(つなひ)き

“Turtle found a long, strong vine.”
