Unit 9·Dictionary Writer's Rulebook📋 summary
📋 Everything a Dictionary Writer must know
THE PATTERN
thing + which + more info
Gravity is a force which pulls you down.
THE FUSION
which replaces it / them and fuses two sentences into one — always jumping to the front of its part.
👻 THE ECHO TRAP
Never repeat the old pronoun: ✗ "which it pulls" ✗ "wear them"
🚪 THE GATE
who = people · which = things · (that can sub for which!)
