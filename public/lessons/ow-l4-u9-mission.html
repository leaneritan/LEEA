⚽ A physicist’s question about soccer

Why does a free kick curve in the air?

💡 Creative

Guess as many reasons as you can. Wind? Spin? The way the boot hits it?

🧠 Critical

Now test them. If it were only wind, would it curve indoors? It does. So what is left?

🔬 The physicist

The spin makes the air move faster on one side than the other — so the ball bends.

💬 In 2025–26 Erling Haaland took the Premier League Golden Boot with 27 goals, and Kylian Mbappé led La Liga with 25. Every one of those shots obeyed the same physics.
