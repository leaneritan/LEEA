🙃 Flip Test

Step 4 says the picture on the back should be upside down. Which one is upside down?

Why does it matter? When you flip the card over to spin it, the back picture turns the right way up again. Draw it normally and it will appear upside down when it spins!

👁️ Trick the Eyes

An optical illusion is a trick on the eyes. Which one is an optical illusion?

👁️ Your eyes are brilliant — but they can be fooled. That is not a fault. It is exactly what makes films and cartoons possible.

👁️
optical illusion
noun phrase
a trick on the eyes — you see something that is not really there
“A thaumatrope creates an optical illusion.”

🛠️ Build it — steps 1 and 2

1
Cut out two circles of cardstock or cardboard. Make them the same size.
2
Draw an object, person, or animal in the middle of one circle.
💡 Draw around a cup or a roll of tape

Two circles the same size — if they do not match, it will wobble.

⚽ Leo’s idea?

A soccer ball on circle one … and a goal on circle two? 🥅

🛠️ Steps 3 and 4 — the tricky one

3
Draw a “home” for the object, person, or animal on the other circle.
4
Stick or glue the two circles together. The picture on the back must be upside down. 🙃
⚠️ Step 4 is where thaumatropes go wrong

Flip the back circle over before you glue. If it is the right way up now, it will be upside down when it spins.

💬 Check the flip together before any glue touches anything.

🛠️ Steps 5 and 6 — the moment of truth

5
Make holes on each side. Attach string or a rubber band. 🔗
6
Spin the circles fast and watch the two pictures turn into one! 🌀
🔮 Now — before you spin

Read your prediction out loud one more time.

💬 Then twist the string tight and let go. 🎉

👁️ The real answer

Your eyes hold on to a picture for a split second after it has gone.

1
The fish disappears as the card turns…
2
…but it is still in your eye for a moment.
3
The tank appears while the fish is still there.
4
So the two pictures seem to combine. 🐠🪟

🔧 Modify — if it is not working

Simpler version

Cut one circle only. Draw one picture on each side. Same effect, half the work.

If it will not spin

Rubber bands beat string. Twist them tight, then pull outwards on both sides.

If the pictures do not line up

Both drawings must be in the middle, and the back one upside down. 🙃
