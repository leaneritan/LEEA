Word 1 unlocked
🎯

toward

Moving closer to something. 🎯

📖 "The girl is running toward the finish line."

Dad 💬 "Walk toward me, Leo."

Leo 💬 "Haaland runs toward the goal — 27 times last season!" ⚽

🇯🇵 Japanese bridge — tap

toward = 〜の方(ほう)へ

In Japanese the little word comes after: ゴールへ.
In English it comes before: toward the goal.

Word 2 unlocked
🚪

away from

Moving further from something. The opposite of toward. 🚪

📖 "The boy is riding away from the others."

Dad 💬 "Now walk away from me."

🇯🇵 Japanese bridge — tap

away from = 〜から離(はな)れて

Japanese needs one small word: 公園から.
English needs two words together: away from. Never just "away the park". 🚫

Word 3 unlocked
🧭

direction

The way something goes or points. 🧭

📖 "The boy and the girl are riding in the same direction."

Dad 💬 "I told Leo which direction to move."

Leo 💬 "Mbappé changed direction and scored — 25 goals in La Liga!" 🇪🇸

🇯🇵 Japanese bridge — tap

direction = 方向(ほうこう)

Japanese has no a or the — so it is easy to forget them.
Say it out loud: "a direction" · "the same direction" · "two directions".

lean 🚴
  • Two uses: lean your body to the side (turning), and lean ON something (resting). The book uses both.
  • Do it: lean right, lean left, then lean on the table — carefully!

🚴

lean

To move your body a little to the side. 🚴

📖 "Don't lean on the table. It might break!"

🇯🇵 Japanese bridge — tap

lean = 傾(かたむ)ける · もたれる

Two jobs in English, one word:
🚴 lean to the left — your body tips
🪑 lean on the table — you rest on it

gravity 🌍
  • Gravity is the force that pulls things down toward the ground.
  • Tap the blue box: gravity is uncountable — never "a gravity", never "gravities".
  • This word also appears in the Reading lesson — Leo will meet it again.
Word 5 unlocked — rack complete!
🌍

gravity

The force which pulls things down toward the Earth. 🌍

📖 "Gravity pulls you toward the center of the Earth."

Dad 💬 "What force makes the pencil fall?"

Leo 💬 "Gravity brings every cross back down!" ⚽

🇯🇵 Japanese bridge — tap

gravity = 重力(じゅうりょく)

You cannot count gravity.
✅ "Gravity pulls it down."
🚫 "A gravity" · 🚫 "gravities"

Sound Lab · Two sounds that fight

Say them after Dad 🔊

/æ/

like in bag

happen backward gravity cat
/ʌ/

like in bug

rub bug wonderful cut

🎯 Today's word gravity uses /æ/. Say it: gra-vi-ty.

Mouth position — the slide that does the work
  • For /æ/ the tongue sits near the front, behind the lower teeth. For /ʌ/ the tongue presses gently down at the back.
  • Tap the switch and have Leo put a finger on his own chin to feel his jaw drop for /æ/.
  • Don't ask him to hear the difference first — ask him to move his tongue. The hearing follows.
Sound Lab · Where is your tongue?

Move your tongue, not your ears 👅

/æ/ — tongue near the front, behind your lower teeth. Jaw drops open. Say cat. 🐱

🇯🇵 Why it is hard — tap

Japanese あ sits between these two English sounds. So cat and cut both come out as キャット／カット.

Fix it with your tongue, not your ear. 👅
