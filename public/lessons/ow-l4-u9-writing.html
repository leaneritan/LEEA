🎯 Purpose Target — so vs. so that

so = what happened

“He hit the ball hard, so it went over the fence.”
Result. It already happened.

so that = the goal you want

“You hit the ball so that it turns around the pole.”
Purpose. That is WHY you do it.

Choose the sentence that shows a goal, not just a result:

🎯
so that
effect word · purpose
shows the goal — the effect you WANT to happen
“You hit the ball so that it turns around the pole.”
〜するように・〜するために

The writer

Isabelle wrote about a game she learned last week: tetherball.

What is tetherball? 🎾

A ball on a rope. The rope is tied to a tall pole. Two players hit the ball in opposite directions to wind the rope around the pole.

Your job while we read

🔵 Find the cause words. 🟢 Find the effect words. Underline all of them.

Paragraph 1 — How she started playing

PARAGRAPH 1 Last week I learned how to play tetherball with my brother. My brother plays a lot, so he taught me. We went to the park because there’s a new tetherball court there.

because → the reason they went to the park

so → the result of her brother playing a lot

Found: 2 connectors 🔍

Paragraph 2 — How you play the game

PARAGRAPH 2 As there aren’t many rules, it’s easy to start playing tetherball. Two people play the game. One person hits the ball to the right. The other person hits it to the left. Because the rope is connected to a pole, the ball can’t fly away. You hit the ball so that it turns around the pole. Tetherball looks easy. But it isn’t.

As → starts the sentence with the reason

Because → the reason the ball can’t fly away

so that → your goal when you hit it

Found: 3 more 🔍  (total 5)

Paragraph 3 — What happened when she played

PARAGRAPH 3 Last week I hit the ball, it flew around in a big circle. As a result, it was easy for my brother to hit it. Then he hit the ball. It went fast and very high so that I couldn’t hit it. So he won every game. Because I want to win next time, I’m going to practice a lot from now on!

Found: 4 more 🔍  — 9 connectors altogether!

💬 Notice the last sentence: she ends with a cause, not an effect. That is allowed! The cause can come first.

Writing Support

a · an · the

Japanese has no articles — so these three tiny words are the easiest ones to forget. 🇯🇵

a / an

any one of a group

Tetherball is a sport.

the

this exact one

The sport is really fun.

The golden rule: first time vs next time

FIRST MENTION → a / an Tetherball is a sport. People hit a ball that’s connected to a pole.
LATER MENTIONS → the The sport is really fun. To play, you make the ball swing around the pole.
💬 Same ball. Same pole. But the second time we already know which one — so we say the.

🎯 Article Check — fill both gaps

“Leo has ____ new soccer ball. ____ ball is black and white.”

💬 Japanese would say ボール both times. English changes the little word. Watch for it in your writing!

⚽ Dad’s model — the same shape, but soccer

Last season I learned how to take penalties with my friend. He practises a lot, so he taught me. We went to the park because there’s a new goal there.

As there aren’t many rules, penalties are easy to start. One player shoots. The other player is the goalkeeper. Because the goal is very big, you should shoot into a corner. You look one way so that the keeper dives the wrong way. Penalties look easy. But they aren’t.

Last week I shot straight down the middle. As a result, it was easy for my friend to save it. So he won every game. Because I want to win next time, I’m going to practise a lot from now on!
