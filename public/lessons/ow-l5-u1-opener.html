⛈️ Be the Expert — About the Photo

1
This is a supercell — a type of storm. ⛈️
2
Its updraft (upward moving wind) rotates over very large areas — sometimes for hours. 🌀
3
The updraft starts rotating where wind speed and direction differ. 💨
4
Supercells can bring very high winds, tornadoes, frequent lightning and large hail. ⚡
💬 So the cloud is not just big — it is turning. That is what makes it dangerous.
