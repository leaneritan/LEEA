🇳🇱 Our World in Context — the Netherlands

1
A small coastal country in Europe, with many canals and rivers. 🏖️
2
Storms and high winds from the North Sea can raise the seawater and flood the land. 🌊
3
After a deadly flood in 1953, the country took action. 🛠️
4
It built sand dunes along the coast to hold back the rising sea. 🏜️
5
It put two automated gates across the Rhine to protect Rotterdam — each gate is the size of the Eiffel Tower! 🗼
💬 That is what “be prepared” means for a whole country — not just a flashlight in a bag. 🔦
