🐝 Sound Lab 2 — same tongue, different throat

/t/ — VOICE OFF 🤫

Tongue touches just behind your top teeth. Drop it and push air out. Only air. Hand on your throat: nothing moves.

/d/ — VOICE ON 🐝

Exactly the same tongue! But now your voice box is on. Hand on your throat: it buzzes!

🇯🇵 You already know this!
た ta → だ da. The ゛ dakuten means “add voice” — that is exactly /t/ → /d/.
The hard part is the end of a word: write / ride. Don’t add an ‘o’ like ライト raito / ライド raido — stop on the sound.
