Be the Expert
  • Japanese bridge. Japanese makes the past with -た -ta every time: 見る → 見た miru → mita. English has the same idea, but many verbs change shape: see → saw, go → went.
  • Common error: I goed outside / I seed a tree (adding -ed to everything). Say -ed is for regular verbs only. Irregular verbs must be learned.
  • Tap the answers after Leo guesses.
