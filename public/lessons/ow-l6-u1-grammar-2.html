Adverbs of emphasis

Put an adverb of emphasis — so, very, really, incredibly, extremely, super, totally — in front of an adjective to make it stronger.

so・very・really・incredibly・extremely・super・totally などの副詞を形容詞の前に置くと、意味を強められます。

subject + be + adverb of emphasis + adjective

Put the adverb before the adjective

  • Skateboarding / exciting / so → Skateboarding is so exciting.
  • That trick / scary / incredibly → That trick is incredibly scary.

look and seem work like be

  • Those pads / silly / super → Those pads look super silly.
  • His helmet / cool / totally → His helmet looks totally cool.
