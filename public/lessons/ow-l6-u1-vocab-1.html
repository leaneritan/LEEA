  • 📏 length — how long something is from one end to the other.

  • 🏍️ motocross — a race on motorcycles or bicycles over rough ground.
  • ⛷️ skiing — the sport of moving over snow on skis.
  • 🤸 flip — to turn over quickly in the air.
  • 🪁 kitesurfing — a water sport where a large kite pulls you across the water on a board.
  • 🎯 skillful — very good at doing something because you have practiced a lot.
  • 💥 crash — to hit something hard and be damaged.
  • 💪 strength — how strong your body is.
  • 🪂 hang-gliding — the sport of flying with a large, light wing.
  • 🎒 equipment — the things you need in order to do an activity.
  • 🚑 accident — something bad that happens without anyone planning it.
  • 🩹 injury — damage to a part of your body.
  • 📐 height — how high something is above the ground.
