Passive voice: Simple past

Use was or were + the past participle when you want to talk about what happened to something, and who did it is unknown or not important.

何がされたかを話し、だれがしたかが分からない、または重要でないときは was / were + 過去分詞 を使います。

subject + was / were + past participle

Match was / were to the subject

  • The Sphinx / build / 2500 BCE → The Sphinx was built around 2500 BCE.
  • The statues / find / 1974 → The statues were found in 1974.

Use the past participle, not the past tense

  • The gold / steal → The gold was stolen.
  • The pottery / break → The pottery was broken.

Make questions by moving was / were

  • the statues / discover / when → When were the statues discovered?
  • the treasure / steal → Was the treasure stolen?
