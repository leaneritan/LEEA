Passive voice: Simple past with by + agent

Add by + the person or thing that did the action when it matters who did it.

だれがしたかが大切なときは、by + 動作主 を加えます。

subject + was / were + past participle + by + agent

From active

The hiker found the mummy. → The mummy was found by the hiker.

The hiker found the mummy.

ハイカーがミイラを見つけました。

When to drop by

leave out by + agent when nobody knows or nobody cares

The treasure was discovered in a field.

その宝物は野原で発見されました。

Turn an active sentence around

  • The hiker found the mummy. → The mummy was found by the hiker.
  • Scientists analyzed the body. → The body was analyzed by scientists.

Keep negatives and questions the same shape

  • The divers didn't find the bottles. → The bottles weren't found by the divers.
  • Did thieves break the pottery? → Was the pottery broken by thieves?

Drop by when it adds nothing

  • Someone discovered the treasure in a field. → The treasure was discovered in a field.
  • People buried the gold long ago. → The gold was buried long ago.
