Listen and repeat · TR 2.2

  • ⚰️ a tomb — a room or building where a dead person is buried.
  • 👑 a ruler — a person like a king or queen who leads a country.
  • 🗿 a statue — a figure of a person or animal made from stone or metal.
  • 🕯️ die — to stop living.
  • 🪦 bury — to put something under the ground and cover it.
  • ⛏️ excavate — to dig carefully in the ground to find old objects.
  • 🧟 a mummy — a dead body that has been kept for thousands of years.
  • 🧊 preserve — to keep something in good condition for a very long time.
  • 🖋️ a tattoo — a picture drawn permanently on someone's skin.
  • ❓ a cause — the reason why something happens.
  • 💰 a treasure — gold, money or objects that are worth a lot.
  • 🔍 discover — to find something for the first time.
  • 🏺 an object — a thing you can see and touch.
  • 🔬 analyze — to study something carefully to find out what it is made of.
  • 🥇 gold — a valuable yellow metal.
  • 🥷 a thief — a person who steals things.
