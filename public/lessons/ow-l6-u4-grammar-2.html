Whatever, whenever, wherever, whoever

Add -ever to what, when, where and who to mean “it does not matter which thing, time, place or person”.

what・when・where・who に -ever をつけると「どれでも・いつでも・どこでも・だれでも」という意味になります。

whatever / whenever / wherever / whoever + subject + verb

whatever = any thing that

  • Do / you can / to save water → Do whatever you can to save water.
  • Drink / water is safe → Drink whatever water is safe.

do

whenever and wherever join time and place

  • Save water / you can → Save water whenever you can.
  • Save water / you go → Save water wherever you go.

whoever can be the subject

  • threw paint down the drain / be in trouble → Whoever threw paint down the drain is in trouble!
  • wastes water / make the drought worse → Whoever wastes water is making the drought worse.
