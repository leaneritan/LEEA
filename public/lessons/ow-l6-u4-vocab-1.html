Listen and repeat · TR 4.2

  • 🏞️ a waterfall — water falling straight down from a high place.
  • 🌧️ wet — covered in water or not dry.
  • 🛁 soak — to leave something in water for a while.
  • 🧂 salt water — water with salt in it, like the sea.
  • 🌊 a sea — a large area of salt water, smaller than an ocean.
  • 🛟 float — to stay on the top of water without sinking.
  • 📏 sea level — the height of the surface of the sea, used to measure other heights.
  • 🏔️ a lake — a large area of water with land all around it.
  • 🧊 a glacier — a very large mass of ice that moves slowly.
  • 🪓 carve — to cut a shape into something hard, slowly.
  • 💧 a drop of water — one very small round amount of water.
  • ❄️ freeze — to become hard and cold, and turn into ice.
  • 🚰 fresh water — water without salt, the kind we can drink.
  • 🐊 a swamp — soft wet land where water covers the ground.
  • 🫗 filter — to pass water through something to take the dirt out.
