Reported speech: Statements

To report what someone said, use said (that) and move the verb one step into the past: is → was, don't → didn't, can → could.

だれかの発言を伝えるときは said (that) を使い、動詞を1つ過去にずらします。is → was、don't → didn't、can → could。

subject + said (that) + subject + past verb
