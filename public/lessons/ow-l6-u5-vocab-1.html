Listen and repeat · TR 5.2

  • 📏 a millimeter — a very small unit of length — one thousandth of a meter.
  • 🔬 a microscope — a tool that makes very small things look much bigger.
  • 😱 horrible — very unpleasant to look at or think about.
  • 🐜 common — happening often or found in many places.
  • 🏞️ habitat — the natural place where a plant or animal lives.
  • 🦠 an organism — any living thing, big or small.
  • 🧫 a cell — the smallest living part of a plant or animal.
  • 🧍 a human — a person.
  • 📉 thin — narrow — not wide or fat.
  • 🤏 grab — to take hold of something quickly.
  • ♂️ male — of the sex that does not have babies in most animals.
  • 📐 a centimeter — a unit of length equal to ten millimeters.
  • ♀️ female — of the sex that has babies in most animals.
