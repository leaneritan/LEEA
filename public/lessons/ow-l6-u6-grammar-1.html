Reported speech: Imperatives

To report an instruction, use told + person + to + base verb. For a negative instruction, use told + person + not to + base verb.

指示を伝えるときは told + 人 + to + 動詞の原形 を使います。否定の指示は told + 人 + not to + 動詞の原形 です。

subject + told + person + (not) to + base verb

Pronouns change

“my toys” → “his toys”

My brother told me not to break his toys.

「私のおもちゃ」は「彼のおもちゃ」に変わります。

No that

never use that with an imperative

They told me to buy another one.

命令文の伝達では that は使いません。

Negative: not goes before to

  • “Don't break my toys.” → My brother told me not to break his toys.
