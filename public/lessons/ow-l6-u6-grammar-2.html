Reported speech: Questions

To report a wh- question, use asked + person + the question word + normal word order. To report a yes/no question, use asked + person + if.

疑問詞のある質問は asked + 人 + 疑問詞 + ふつうの語順 で伝えます。yes / no の質問は asked + 人 + if を使います。

subject + asked + person + what / if + subject + past verb

Wh- question

asked + person + what / where / how + subject + verb

Lisa asked me what she needed to do next.

リサは次に何をすればよいかたずねました。

Yes/no question

asked + person + if + subject + verb

Ken asked me if I could help him.

ケンは手伝ってもらえるかたずねました。

No question mark

the reported question ends with a full stop

Ken asked me if I could help him.

伝えられた質問はピリオドで終わります。

Normal word order

no do / does / did in the reported clause

She asked me what I wanted.

伝えるときは do / does / did を使いません。

Backshift

can → could, is → was, do → did

Did you ask the store if it was waterproof?

can は could、is は was になります。

Use if for a yes/no question

  • “Can you help me?” → Ken asked me if I could help him.
  • “Is it waterproof?” → I asked the store if it was waterproof.

Keep the question word for a wh- question

  • “What do I need to do next?” → Lisa asked me what she needed to do next.
  • “How much do you want to spend?” → The clerk asked me how much I wanted to spend.
Our World Level 6 · Unit 6 · Student Book audio script
do

Drop do, does and did

  • “What do you want?” → She asked me what I wanted.
  • “Where did you buy it?” → He asked me where I had bought it.
