Listen and read · TR 6.1

Have you ever bought a product that broke the next day? Probably not! One reason is that it's bad for the manufacturer if their product breaks. Also, unhappy customers tell others if a product isn't good quality!

The crash test

🚗

Cars must pass safety tests. To test a car, manufacturers put dummies, which look like humans, inside the car. Then they make the car crash. Crash tests show manufacturers what happens on impact.

車は安全性の検査に合格しなければなりません。製造業者は人間に似たダミー人形を車に乗せ、衝突させます。衝突試験は衝撃で何が起こるかを示します。

The torque test

🧸

The “torque test” twists toys until they break. The “drop test” drops toys on the floor!

「トルク試験」はおもちゃが壊れるまでねじります。「落下試験」はおもちゃを床に落とします！

The water test

💦

Waterproof toys are dipped in water. Machines tear balloons. If something doesn't work, manufacturers fix the problem.

防水のおもちゃは水に浸されます。機械が風船を引き裂きます。うまくいかなければ、製造業者は問題を直します。

Why bother?

🙋

It can cost a lot of money to replace a broken product, and unhappy customers tell others if a product isn't good quality.

壊れた商品を交換するには多くの費用がかかります。また、品質が悪ければ不満な客がほかの人に伝えます。
