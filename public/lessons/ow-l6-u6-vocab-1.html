Listen and repeat · TR 6.2

  • 📦 a product — something that is made to be sold.
  • 💔 break — to come apart into pieces or stop working.
  • 🏭 a manufacturer — a company that makes products.
  • 💵 cost — to have a particular price.
  • 🛍️ a customer — a person who buys something from a shop or company.
  • ⭐ quality — how good or bad something is.
  • 🧪 test — to try something out to see if it works well.
  • 🦺 safety — being free from danger.
  • 🧍 a dummy — a model of a person used for testing.
  • 💥 a crash test — a test where a car is crashed on purpose.
  • 🎯 impact — the moment one thing hits another.
  • ⬇️ drop — to let something fall.
  • 💧 waterproof — not letting water in.
  • 🫗 dip — to put something into a liquid for a moment.
  • 📄 tear — to pull something apart so it rips.
  • 🔧 fix — to repair something that is broken.
