Angel Falls · TR 7.8

Imagine searching for gold, but instead of finding it, you see one of the great wonders of the world. That's what happened to Jimmie Angel.

Paragraph 1

Jimmie loved to fly. A miner hired him to fly to Venezuela in search of gold. In 1933, during one of his flights, he saw an amazing waterfall.

Paragraph 3

After this adventure, the falls were named in Jimmie's honor: Angel Falls. Angel Falls is located in southeast Venezuela in Canaima National Park. It's the tallest waterfall in the world. A column of water falls almost 980 meters from the top of the mountain to its base.

Reading strategy — comparing sizes

The passage compares Angel Falls with buildings you may know: 62 meters taller than the Burj Khalifa, almost twice as tall as the Shanghai World Financial Center. When a text compares, ask what is being compared with what.

この文章はエンジェルフォールを有名な建物と比べています。比較が出てきたら「何と何を比べているのか」を確かめましょう。
