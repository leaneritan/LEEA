Listen and repeat · TR 7.2

  • 🧗 ascend — to go up.
  • 🪜 descend — to go down.
  • 🌊 underwater — below the surface of the water.
  • 🎒 safety gear — the equipment that keeps you safe.
  • 🔦 a headlamp — a lamp you wear on your head.
  • 💡 shine — to give out light, or point a light at something.
  • 🪢 a rope — a thick strong cord.
  • 🤔 curiosity — the wish to find out about things.
  • 😲 by accident — without planning it.
  • 🤕 trip over — to catch your foot on something and fall.
  • 📍 locate — to find exactly where something is.
  • 🌉 cross — to go from one side of something to the other.
  • 🏞️ a stream — a small narrow river.
  • 🕳️ a tunnel — a long narrow passage under the ground.
  • 🏃 chase — to run after someone or something.
  • ⚠️ a risk — the chance that something bad will happen.
