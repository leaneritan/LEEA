Wish statements

Use wish + a past-tense verb to talk about something that is not true now but that you would like. Use were for every subject after wish.

今そうではないけれど、そうであってほしいことは wish + 過去形 で表します。wish のあとの be動詞はどの主語でも were を使います。

subject + wish(es) + subject + past verb / could / were

Negative wish

wish + subject + didn't + base verb

Dad wishes the vacuuming robot didn't cost so much. (But it costs a lot.)

父は掃除ロボットがそんなに高くなければいいのにと思っています。（でも高いのです。）

Third person

he / she / it + wishes

Mom wishes she had a robot.

he / she / it には wishes を使います。

Use were, not was

  • my teacher / be a robot → I wish my teacher were a robot.
  • I / be a robot → I wish I were a robot!
Our World Level 6 · Unit 8 · Student Book audio script
do

can becomes could

  • I / program a robot → I wish I could program a robot.
  • I / fix myself → I wish I could fix myself.

Every wish hides an opposite

  • Mom / have a robot that plays table tennis → Mom wishes she had a robot that played table tennis.
  • Dad / the robot not cost so much → Dad wishes the vacuuming robot didn't cost so much.
