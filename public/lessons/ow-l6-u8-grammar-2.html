Passive voice: Future

Use will be or won't be + the past participle to say what will or will not be done to something in the future.

未来に何がされるか／されないかは will be / won't be + 過去分詞 で表します。

subject + will (not) be + past participle

will be + past participle

  • robots / program / to do dangerous jobs → Robots will be programmed to do dangerous jobs.
  • the room / clean / by a domestic robot → The room will be cleaned by a domestic robot.

won't be for the negative

  • most people / not require / to work long hours → Most people won't be required to work long hours.
  • that job / not do / by a human → That job won't be done by a human.
