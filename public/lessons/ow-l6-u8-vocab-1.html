Listen and repeat · TR 8.2

  • 🛸 science fiction — stories about the future, space or imagined technology.
  • 🛞 mobile — able to move around easily.
  • 🎯 precise — exact and correct in every detail.
  • 🕹️ control — to make something do what you want.
  • 📱 a remote control — a device that operates a machine from a distance.
  • 📋 a task — a piece of work that has to be done.
  • 🗣️ social — to do with being with other people.
  • 🐕 a companion — someone or something that stays with you for company.
  • 🧩 a feature — an important part or quality of something.
  • ☢️ dangerous — likely to cause harm.
  • 🧠 complex — having many parts and difficult to understand.
  • 💬 respond to — to answer or react to something.
  • 📡 a command — an instruction given to a person or machine.
  • 📊 information — facts about something.
  • 💻 program — to give a machine a set of instructions to follow.
