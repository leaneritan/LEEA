Listen and repeat · TR 8.5

  • 🎙️ voice recognition — the ability of a machine to understand speech.
  • 🙂 facial recognition — the ability of a machine to know a person by their face.
  • ⚙️ mechanical — worked by a machine, not by muscles.
  • 📶 a sensor — a part that lets a machine detect light, touch or sound.
  • 🔴 a laser — a very narrow, strong beam of light.
