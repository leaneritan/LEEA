It's + adjective + infinitive

Use It's or It isn't + an adjective + to + the base verb to judge an action — to say it is important, useful, safe, legal or not.

It's / It isn't + 形容詞 + to + 動詞の原形 で、その行動が大切か、役に立つか、安全か、合法かを判断して言います。

Opposite adjectives

It's + un-/in-/im-/il- adjective + to + base verb

It's unsafe to be a pirate.

海賊になるのは危険です。

Useful adjectives

important / useful / necessary / helpful / right

It's necessary to know how to swim.

泳ぎ方を知っていることは必要です。

It points forward to the to-phrase

  • know how to swim / important → It's important to know how to swim.
  • know how to swim / useful → It's useful to know how to swim.

Two ways to say the same thing

  • take artifacts / not legal → It isn't legal to take artifacts.
  • be a pirate / illegal → It's illegal to be a pirate.
Our World Level 6 · Unit 9 · Student Book audio script
do

Always the base form after to

  • know how many drowned / impossible → It's impossible to know how many drowned.
  • throw trash into the ocean / illegal → It's illegal to throw trash into the ocean.
