Listen and repeat · TR 9.2

  • 🤿 dive — to go down into water head first, or deep under water.
  • 🚢 a shipwreck — a ship that has been destroyed or sunk at sea.
  • 🌊 sink — to go down under the surface of water.
  • 📦 cargo — the goods carried by a ship or plane.
  • ⚔️ a weapon — something used for fighting or attacking.
  • 🥈 silver — a valuable shiny grey metal.
  • 🏴‍☠️ a pirate — someone who attacks and robs ships at sea.
  • 🪝 capture — to catch and take control of someone or something.
  • 🧑‍✈️ a captain — the person in charge of a ship.
  • ⚓ a sailor — someone who works on a ship.
  • 📖 a legend — an old story that people tell but cannot prove.
  • 🧊 an iceberg — a very large piece of ice floating in the sea.
  • 🛶 a lifeboat — a small boat used to save people from a sinking ship.
  • 🌊 drown — to die under water because you cannot breathe.
  • 🧳 a passenger — someone travelling on a ship, plane or bus.
  • 👥 a crew — all the people who work on a ship or plane.
