Listen and repeat · TR 9.5

  • ✅ correct — right, with no mistakes.
  • ❌ incorrect — not right — the opposite of correct.
  • 🦺 safe — not in danger.
  • ⚠️ unsafe — dangerous — the opposite of safe.
  • ⚖️ legal — allowed by the law.
  • 🚫 illegal — against the law — the opposite of legal.
  • 🙆 possible — able to happen or be done.
  • 🙅 impossible — not able to happen — the opposite of possible.
